About Madox Hospital

Learn more about our hospital, mission, vision and values.

Hospital Background

Madox Hospital is a specialized hospital based in Nyeri, Kenya.

Our hospital is committed to providing professional, respectful and dependable healthcare services to patients.

We provide several specialized services including maternity, pharmacy, surgery, therapy, radiology, laboratory services and consultation.

Our Purpose

Our purpose is to provide patients with quality specialized healthcare in a safe, clean and professional environment.

Our Mission

To provide safe, respectful and patient-focused specialized healthcare through skilled professionals and reliable support services.

Our Vision

To become a trusted specialized hospital known for professional healthcare and a positive patient experience.

Our Commitment

We are committed to protecting patient dignity, promoting safety and providing clear communication.

Our Core Values

These values guide the way Madox Hospital serves its patients.

  • Compassion: Treating patients with kindness and respect.
  • Professionalism: Maintaining high standards of conduct.
  • Safety: Promoting patient safety in healthcare.
  • Teamwork: Working together to provide better care.
  • Integrity: Communicating honestly and responsibly.
  • Quality: Providing reliable healthcare services.
